###### THIS IS OUR MOTTO FOR WELL-BEING  `<h4>`

Dragonfly Village, an enchanted retreat paradise, is set in the midst of one of the most beautiful rice field walks in the center of Ubud, Bali. Tranquility and stunning greenery all around… panorama with sacred Mt Agung and the northern ranges of mountain in a distance… open space filled with dragonflies in the day and fireflies in the night… symphony of crickets and frogs… for those who enjoy being in the nature, healing and learning. Only footsteps away from center Ubud, but tucked away from its hassles and bustles, we offer the best of both worlds.
Our structures are mostly recycled wood and traditional antique teak houses brought over from remote villages in Java. We focus our energy on living simply and consciously within an organic environment.
You will find various workshops on yoga retreats, bodywork, detox & fasting programs and other healing modalities being hosted at Dragonfly Village.
We warmly welcome you to join us in sharing this space for individual and collective transformation.

#### FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS  `<h2>`

[LINK/BUTTON] "Do you organize airport or other location pick up / drop off ?" → 
Yes just send us your flight info or location.
[LINK/BUTTON] "How do we get our luggage into the Dragonfly in the small path ?" → 
Our staff are used to transferring luggage on motorbike. Just call our reception 5 mins before you arrive.
[LINK/BUTTON] "Do I need torch light ?" → 
Yes it’s a good idea. The path to the Dragonfly is not lit at night, useful to have a torch light with you.
[LINK/BUTTON] "Is there Air-con ?" → 
We are an eco friendly set up, there are no air-cons in our rooms, only ceiling fan in every bungalow. Ubud is slightly cooler in the night due to terrain elevation. If you need extra table fan, please request at our reception.
[LINK/BUTTON] "What food do you offer ?" → 
Our kitchen is vegetarian only. Vegan & GF options readily available on requests. Breakfast, lunch and diner daily from 7.30am – 9pm.
[LINK/BUTTON] "When is sauna open ? What do I have to bring ? Do I have to book ?" → 
Our herbal steam sauna is open to the general public from 6pm-9pm every Monday (silent), Wednesday and Saturday. No reservation required. Please bring your swimming suits and towels. We provide sarong for you to borrow.
[LINK/BUTTON] "Can I leave luggage in the Dragonfly ?" → 
Yes, you can leave your luggage in our reception if you arrive earlier than check in time 2pm and leave later than our check out time 11am.
[LINK/BUTTON] "Do you organise tours ?" → 
Yes, we can assist to organize outings and tours for you.
[LINK/BUTTON] "What other services do you have ?" → 
Laundry and massage service is available.
