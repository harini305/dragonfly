### Georgios  `<h1>`

A fairytale among the rice fields If you are looking for a fairytale, this is the right place for you! Magnificent gardens with a small river like pond running through them. At night there are fire bugs greeting you, geckos singing, frogs singing… if you are lucky you can here the rain on the roof- all that matched up with great stuff always ready to meet your needs. And don’t underestimate the peace that you are going to find between the rice fields and the friendly locals!
