### Jayne Burns-Milostic  `<h1>`

Dragonfly Village is a beautiful village surrounded by rice paddies. Minutes walk to bustling Ubud but when you are in the village you are surrounded by peace and tranquility. The people that work there are so kind and helpful, nothing is too much trouble. The food is prepared with absolute love and nourishes your soul as well as your body. I could not recommend it highly enough!! I spent a life-changing 5 days here in retreat and would love to return with my family for a holiday. I loved everything about this place.
